Section 1

How this map is organized

A single practice that designs how people and organizations learn, expressed across four connected areas of expertise and a dedicated accessibility service line — each with its own node and its own page. Every contribution below sits in its home area and links to the others it draws on, because the areas are not separate skills but one body of work seen from different perspectives.

Each contribution below sits in one home area and names the other contributions it draws on. The interactive version arranges the same material in two ways: by area, which shows where each contribution lives, and by affinity, which repositions contributions by how alike they are, so relationships across areas become visible. This page presents the same content in reading order.

A small number of contributions belong to two areas at once. Each appears once here, under its home area, and states its second membership. An area page may therefore list more resources than this page groups under that area.

Section 2

The five areas

Each area has its own page. The accessibility work is offered as a service line through Accessible Learning Labs.

Accessibility

A dedicated service line, offered through Accessible Learning Labs, that reviews and designs learning environments for genuine accessibility — holding standards, inclusive design, and systems knowledge together rather than treating access as a final checklist.

Grounding. WCAG; UDL; inclusive design

Visit the Accessibility page · Read its 3 contributions below

Knowledge Mobilization

The design of how research and evidence reach the people who can use them, and change what they do. This area gathers the contributions that turn knowledge into usable, evaluated, and sustained practice across sectors.

Grounding. Knowledge mobilization; implementation science

Visit the Knowledge Mobilization page · Read its 10 contributions below

Learning Experience Design

The design of learning as it is actually experienced — how a learner moves through it, engages with it, and makes it their own. This area gathers the contributions that shape the experience itself, from motivation through practice, reflection, and the journey that holds them together.

Grounding. Experiential learning; human-centred design

Visit the Learning Experience Design page · Read its 10 contributions below

Learning Systems Design

The design of learning as an organizational system — its analysis, infrastructure, evaluation, and change — rather than as isolated programs. This area gathers the contributions that situate learning inside institutions and help them move.

Grounding. Systems thinking; organizational learning

Visit the Learning Systems Design page · Read its 14 contributions below

Universal Design for Learning

Designing proactively for the full, predictable range of human variability, so learning fits diverse learners from the outset rather than being retrofitted. This area gathers the contributions that reduce barriers and widen access without lowering rigour.

Grounding. Universal Design for Learning (CAST)

Visit the Universal Design for Learning page · Read its 10 contributions below
